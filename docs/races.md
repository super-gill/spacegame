# Races and manufacturers

Status: agreed design, 3 November 2026. The full version, with every manufacturer's house style and a coverage matrix, is the "Spacegame: Races and Manufacturers" doc; the history and politics are in "Spacegame: Lore of the Known Universe". This page is the summary the code is built against. Names are placeholders.

## Structure

Race, then manufacturer, then product. The race sets the colours, the shape language, the tech its hulls are bonused for, and its navy rules. The manufacturer sets which industries it builds for, its accent within the race look, where it sells, and whether it supplies the navy. A few multi-race firms work everywhere but never build warships.

## Passenger transport

Its own industry, separate from cargo: taxis and private shuttles (Meridian), buses and commuter shuttles (Meridian, Tessaract), pilgrim liners and cruise ships (Grand Tithe, Tessaract). Passenger cabins are a module family.

## Decisions

- **Pilots** pick a race at character creation and start in that race's home system. The race is an origin and starting standing, not bonuses.
- **Navy ships** need standing and a licence with the race, and come with duties that grow with access. Owned ships are never taken back; a pilot who abandons their duty becomes a traitor to that race. Militaries are mostly private pilots flying under a race's banner.
- **Manufacturer standing** grows by working with a maker and unlocks discounts and special hulls and modules. It is never lost for flying a rival's ships.
- **The Aurel** are one people with no manufacturers. Each ship is crafted individually and sealed. They live in their own hard-to-reach region. Not obtainable by normal means to begin with.
- **Support and repair** is Vael-only for now (the lore's Mercy Clause makes the Vael sell to everyone).
- **Stations** are race-specific in look; the hub is much bigger than its pads; most stations have only a few pads; each race's home system has one enormous capital station.
